How does payment work?

After selecting an available time for a paid session, you are redirected to Stripe's secure checkout. Your booking is confirmed only after successful payment verification. Stripe can send your payment receipt by email when receipts are enabled in the Stripe Dashboard.

Where do sessions take place?

Sessions are designed for online delivery. Connection details or meeting arrangements are provided separately after your appointment is confirmed.

Can I discuss my learning goals first?

Yes. You can request a complimentary 20-minute introductory consultation, subject to availability.

What if I need to change my appointment?

Please contact ITECCS as early as possible. Changes and refunds are handled under the terms confirmed by ITECCS; they are not automated on this page.
