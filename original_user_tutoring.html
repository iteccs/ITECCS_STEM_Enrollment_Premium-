Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Who is tutoring designed for?

High school, AP, and university students seeking personalized support in mathematics, from algebra to advanced undergraduate subjects.

How are sessions conducted?

Sessions take place online with live discussion, screen sharing, and digital handwriting for equations and worked examples. Meeting details are shared after booking.

How much does a tutoring session cost?

A private 60-minute tutoring session costs $100. The introductory 20-minute consultation is complimentary.

How do I book and pay?

Once online booking is activated, choose an available time and complete secure payment through Stripe before your paid session is confirmed.

Can I ask about a subject not listed?
